# Color Palette Training — Petaluma Flip Curb Appeal

This is the locked exterior palette for the Petaluma flip. Every future design
mockup, image generation, and material recommendation must pull from this
palette so all renderings look like the SAME house with the SAME design, not a
new concept each time.

## Fixed / existing colors (do not change, design around them)

| Element | Color | Approx. hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| House siding | Warm beige | `#C9B99B` | Existing. All accents chosen to contrast with this. |
| Concrete path & driveway | Aged gray | `#A8A29A` | Existing. Keep clean; grass softens its edges. |
| White brick planter border | Stark white | `#EDEAE3` | Being HIDDEN by redwood cladding — should not appear in "after" mockups. |

## Hero material palette (the design)

| Element | Color | Approx. hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Redwood planter cladding | Warm redwood brown | `#96522E` – `#A65E38` | The focal feature. Rich, warm, freshly finished (clear or lightly toned sealer). Never gray/weathered in mockups. |
| Bark mulch | Dark chocolate brown | `#3E2C20` | 2–3" deep, uniform. Dark mulch is deliberate — it makes plant greens and lavender purple pop. |
| Grass patch | Fresh mid-green | `#4E8A3C` | Only in the small planter-box area by the path. Healthy, mowed, not neon. |
| Rock border (grass edge only) | Neutral gray/tan river rock | `#9B9285` / `#B5A98F` | Single low row around the grass patch ONLY. No rock anywhere else. |

## Planting color layers (inside the redwood box)

| Layer | Plants | Color role | Approx. hex |
|---|---|---|---|
| Back (structure) | Upright rosemary / Ceanothus / columnar evergreen | Deep evergreen green | `#2F4A2E` |
| Middle (color pop) | Lavender + white/pink bloomers | Lavender purple, soft white, blush pink | `#8B7FC7`, `#F5F1E8`, `#E8B4C0` |
| Front (texture) | Succulents (echeveria, sedum, aeonium), Dymondia | Silver-green, blue-green | `#8FA88C`, `#6E8B74` |

| Element | Color options | Approx. hex |
|---|---|---|
| Front door | Navy, charcoal, or warm black | `#1F3A5F`, `#3A3F45`, `#26221E` |
| House numbers / fixtures | Matte black or brushed metal | `#26221E` |

## Palette rules (why mockups drifted before)

1. **One palette, every render.** The earlier mockups changed materials and
   tones between images, which made iterations feel random. Reuse the hexes
   above so each new image reads as a revision of the same design.
2. **Warm vs. cool balance**: warm redwood + dark mulch (warm base) against
   silver-green foliage + lavender purple (cool accents). Don't add more warm
   accent colors (no red flowers, no orange gravel) — it muddies the contrast.
3. **White is for blooms only.** The white brick is being covered; the only
   white in the "after" look is small flowers and trim.
4. **Rejected looks — never render again**: river-rock edging around mulch or
   lining the whole path; bulky rock borders; large lawn areas; gray/weathered
   wood on the planter box.
5. **Listing-photo test**: dark mulch + fresh redwood + purple/silver planting
   against beige siding is the highest-contrast, best-photographing combo.
   If a proposed change reduces that contrast, flag it before rendering.
