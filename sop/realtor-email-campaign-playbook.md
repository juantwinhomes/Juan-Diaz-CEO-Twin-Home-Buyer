# Realtor Email Campaign — Strategy & Action Plan

**Campaign:** "Realtors July 2026" (Instantly, ID `67d3a54b`) · **Owner:** Carlo (replies), Seth (oversight)
**Status as of Jul 15, 2026:** Active 4 sending days · 1,908 leads · 100 sent · 0 real replies · 10 bounces (10%)
**Decision:** Pause → clean → resume within 2–3 days. $0 spend until the channel earns it.

## Strategy (the frame)

This is a **referral-network build, not a blast.** Goal: 50 Bay Area agents who
think "Twin Home Buyer" when a listing is too ugly, too slow, or too distressed
to list traditionally — we close cash, agent keeps commission. One agent
referring two deals/year beats a thousand opens. Optimize for **conversations
booked**, not emails sent. The channel only needs to work twice to pay for
itself for years.

## What was wrong at launch (root causes, not results)

1. **Unverified list** → 10% bounce rate (danger line ~3%). Per-send reputation
   damage with Google; compounds daily.
2. **Cold sends from the real company domain** (twinhomebuyer.com) including
   juan@ — the inbox that talks to lenders, sellers, attorney.
3. **No suppression list** — active business contacts (e.g. Guillermo Reyes,
   agent on 751 27th Ave) sitting in the cold list.
4. **One-touch sequence** — no follow-ups; most cold replies come on touches 2–4.
5. **Fake scoreboard** — Instantly counted Kristine's staging thread as
   2 replies + a $1,000 opportunity. Real cold results: 100 sent, 0 replies.

**Not** wrong: zero replies in week one. Four sending days, single touch — not
a verdict. Channel gets judged at the 500-send gate below.

## Decision gates (no spend until earned)

- **Test-batch gates (Juan's, govern the 50–100 lead relaunch):** bounce must be
  <2% on the cleaned list; if bounce is clean but replies are still 0 after
  ~150–200 verified sends → revisit messaging/positioning with real data. Do
  NOT rewrite copy before then — messaging is unproven, not disproven.
- **Scale gates (govern the full-list rollout), at 500 clean sends with follow-ups live (~4–6 weeks):**
  - **Scale trigger:** ≥2% reply rate AND ≥5 real agent conversations (or 1
    referred deal) → buy 2 lookalike sending domains (~$50), move all cold
    volume off twinhomebuyer.com permanently, scale to 100–150/day.
  - **Fix trigger:** below that → change copy or list before any more sends/spend.
- **Kill discipline:** if the channel can't produce conversations after a copy
  + list fix cycle, kill it free of charge. No zombie mode.
- **Success metric:** first referred contract within 60 days of resume.
