# SOP — Using the Brain of Juan Diaz (Team)

> This is the step-by-step *how*. For what the Brain is for and the lines it
> won't cross — the 70% rule, "reviewer not worker," escalate-to-humans — read
> `sop/SOP-ask-juan-ai.md` first. The two docs agree: **you do the work; the
> Brain pressure-tests it.**

**Purpose:** Before anything goes to Juan — an update, a question, a report, a
draft — you run it past the **Brain** first. Do your thinking, make your
recommendation, *then* the Brain answers the way Juan would, tells you what's
missing, and helps you sharpen it. Result: Juan gets clean, decision-ready
items, and you get fewer "no, redo this" replies. The Brain reviews your
work — it does not do your work for you.

**Who uses this:** Everyone — Cherry, Kristine, Roiz, Jose, Seth, the PH team.

**Main rule:** 🧠 **Brain first, Juan second.** If it's going to Juan, it goes
through the Brain before it goes to him. (Exception: real emergencies — see
Step 6.)

---

## Step 1 — Open the Brain

- Web: **claude.ai/code** → open the repo `Juan-Diaz-CEO-Twin-Home-Buyer`
- Or on a computer with it installed: open the `JuanBrain` folder → type `claude`

The Brain loads Juan's knowledge automatically. You don't need to explain the
company to it. It already knows the deals, the numbers, the rules, and Juan.

## Step 2 — Give context first, then ask

Paste your raw material FIRST (the email, the numbers, the screenshot, the
situation). THEN ask your question in one or two lines. Example:

> *[paste the lender email]*
> "I need to reply to this today. Draft it, and tell me what Juan would flag."

Don't write long explanations. Raw material + short ask = best answer.

## Step 3 — Ask for the pre-check (the important part)

Before you finalize anything for Juan, ask the Brain:

> **"Review this like Juan will. What's missing? What will he ask me?"**

The Brain checks it against Juan's standards:
- ❓ Is the **answer first**, details after?
- 📅 Is there a **date and an owner** for every action? ("85% done" = rejected.
  "Live Friday 5 PM, Cherry validates Thursday" = accepted.)
- 🔢 Are the **numbers real** and checked? One wrong number = Juan distrusts all of it.
- 🎯 Does it follow the **90-day reset**? (Cash first · 820 28th St focus ·
  no scatter · >$1–2K needs review)
- 🏠 Deal items: does it pass the **buy-box**? (East Bay sub-$1M ✅ · rehab
  >25–30% 🚫 · never past 85%)
- ✍️ Copy/messages: simple, direct, honest — never salesy, never overpromising.

## Step 4 — Fix it, then confirm-back

Apply the Brain's feedback. Then use Juan's confirm-back rule — one line at
the top of anything you send him:

> "**My understanding:** [one sentence]. **My ask:** [one sentence]."

## Step 5 — Send to Juan in HIS format

For updates and recommendations, always this structure:

1. **Issue** — what happened (one line)
2. **Impact** — why it matters (numbers)
3. **Options** — the choices
4. **Recommendation** — pick ONE
5. **Decision needed** — exactly what you need from Juan, by when

Keep it under 10 lines. Juan reads answers, not essays.

## Step 6 — When to skip the Brain and go straight to Juan

- Money leaving the company unexpectedly
- Legal threat, lawsuit, or a lender/escrow emergency
- A seller or deal about to be lost TODAY

Even then: message Juan first, run it through the Brain in parallel.

## What the Brain can also do for you

These are supports *after* you've done the thinking — not a way to skip it
(see the 70% rule in `sop/SOP-ask-juan-ai.md`). Bring your draft or your call
first, then use the Brain to sharpen it:

- Sharpen emails, texts, and scripts **in Juan's voice** (ask for tone variants)
- Explain any deal, number, or rule in the knowledge base ("why was this
  campaign flagged?")
- Stress-test your checklists, training steps, and role prompts
- Answer design questions via the **Designer** (paint codes, finishes, tiers)

## What the Brain will NOT do

- Send anything external by itself — a human always approves
- Approve spending, offers, or legal items — that's Juan (and >$1–2K needs
  attorney/CPA review per the reset)
- Accept "almost done," vague owners, or unchecked numbers — it will push back,
  because Juan would

## Quick example (real pattern)

❌ **Wrong way (straight to Juan):** "Hi Juan, the KPI tracker is 85% done,
just polishing. Will update you soon."

✅ **Right way (through the Brain):** Brain flags: no date, no owner, no
definition of done. You send instead: "KPI tracker live **Monday 9 AM**.
Tracks mail→lead→offer→contract→closed by campaign and area. Cherry validates
data Friday. Need nothing from you — FYI."

That version gets a 👍 in 10 seconds. That's the whole point.
