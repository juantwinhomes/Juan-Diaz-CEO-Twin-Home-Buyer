# SOP — Cleaning & House/Office Upkeep

> **Purpose:** so cleaning runs on a standard, and **Juan doesn't get asked
> about routine items.** ("SLP" in Juan's voice notes = this SOP.) If it's on
> this SOP, the team just does it — no approval needed.
>
> **Owner of the work:** Sonia (execution) · **Tracker:** Kristine · **Juan:**
> not involved in routine cleaning — only sees exceptions.

## 1. Sonia's daily house routine (no need to ask Juan)
Do inside first, then outside. Every day:
1. **Kitchen** — dishes done, counters wiped, sink clean
2. **Beds** made
3. **Quick tidy / organize** — house presentable
4. **Landscaping / garden** — daily upkeep, especially flagged areas
5. **Send photos** to Kristine when done (proof loop — required daily)

This is a *light* surface clean. Sonia does not need Juan's input to do it.

## 2. Prioritization rule (when multiple areas/locations compete)
Do them in this order:
1. **Perishable / health** — fridge, food, spills, anything that rots or smells
2. **Work-critical** — office / workspace / where the business runs
3. **Daily upkeep** — the routine light clean above
4. **Living spaces** — rooms Juan uses (movie room, playroom, living areas)
5. **Big project areas** — basement, garage, full garden — scheduled over days

> Example call: fridge/food beats the office; office beats the daily tidy;
> basement is a scheduled project, not a same-day task.

## 3. Deep-clean projects (basement, movie room, garden overhauls, etc.)
- These are **projects**, not daily tasks — put them on a **checklist** with a
  target day (see `juan-house-cleaning-checklist.md`), don't cram into one day.
- Assign a day/area, send photos on completion, Kristine checks it off.

## 4. Proof & tracking
- **Photos required** on every completed area, daily → Kristine.
- Kristine keeps the checklist current and only surfaces **blockers** to Juan.
- "Done" = photo received, not "I did it."

## 5. When to involve Juan (rare)
- Something's **broken / needs a repair or purchase** (>$1–2K → reset review).
- A **one-off request** outside the routine that isn't on a checklist yet.
- Otherwise: **don't ask Juan** — that's the whole point of this SOP.

## 6. Same rule applies to the office
Office = Juan's workspace / computer area. Keep it clean and functional on the
same standard; it ranks as "work-critical" in the priority rule above.
