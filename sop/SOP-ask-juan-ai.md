# SOP — How & When to Use Ask Juan AI

**Twin Home Buyer | Equity Track Inc.** · Version 1.0 · Internal Use Only

> Companion to `sop/SOP-using-juans-brain.md`. That doc is the step-by-step
> *how*. This doc is the *discipline*: what Ask Juan AI is for, and the lines it
> does not cross.

## Purpose

Ask Juan AI is the company's **CEO Brain**. It helps you think the way Juan
thinks *before* you make an important decision. It reviews your thinking,
challenges assumptions, finds blind spots, and predicts how Juan would react to
your work. **It reviews your work — it does not do your work.**

## Mission

Help every employee think like a CEO instead of just completing tasks. It
improves decisions. It does not replace decision makers.

## What Ask Juan AI IS

- A CEO-level strategic reviewer.
- Trained on Juan's acquisition philosophy, buy-box, priorities, communication
  style, and leadership mindset.
- Used to pressure-test recommendations before they reach Juan.
- A way to surface risks, missing information, and better strategies.

## What Ask Juan AI is NOT

- Juan himself.
- A replacement for your manager.
- An approval system.
- A task executor, virtual assistant, or CRM operator.
- A coding assistant.
- A research assistant that does your homework from scratch.

## Core Principle

Ask Juan AI reviews your work. It should never become the person doing your
work. **Gather → analyze → recommend → then ask for feedback.**

## Correct Workflow

1. Do the work.
2. Analyze the facts.
3. Make your recommendation.
4. Ask Ask Juan AI for strategic feedback.
5. Improve your work.
6. Present the final version to Juan.

## The 70% Rule

Complete **at least 70% of the work** before using Ask Juan AI. Bring a
recommendation, not a blank page.

## When TO Use It

- Deal strategy
- Seller strategy and communication
- Market analysis
- Lead prioritization
- Executive reports
- SOP reviews
- Operational strategy
- Revenue prioritization
- Before presenting work to Juan

## When NOT to Use It

Do not use it to:

- Do your entire job.
- Run comps from scratch.
- Create complete SOPs from nothing.
- Fill out REI BlackBook.
- Perform research you should complete yourself.
- Make final legal, HR, or financial decisions.

## Technical Work Policy

**Ask Juan AI is not a technical AI.** Do not ask it to build automations, write
code, debug software, create APIs, build Make.com / Zapier workflows, configure
GoHighLevel, build dashboards, or write SQL/Python.

Ask the business questions instead:

- Should we build this?
- Is this worth the investment?
- Would Juan prioritize this?
- Does this solve the right business problem?

After a technical solution is built, ask:

- Would Juan approve this?
- Does it solve the business problem?
- Did we over-engineer it?
- What would Juan improve?

> Note: the brain repo does contain build tools (the **Designer** agent, the
> Grok parser in `tools/`). Those are internal build utilities operated by the
> tech/design function — not "Ask Juan AI." Keep the two separate: Ask Juan AI
> judges *whether* to build; the tools *do* the building.

## Best Prompt Format

1. Situation
2. Facts and evidence
3. Your recommendation
4. Decision needed
5. Constraints

Finish with:

> *"Separate facts from assumptions. Tell me what's missing. What would Juan
> challenge first?"*

## Escalate to a Human

Always escalate — Ask Juan AI does not decide these:

- Legal matters
- Contract approval
- Hiring or termination
- Financial commitments
- High-risk operational decisions
- Final purchase approvals

## Golden Rules

- ✓ Do your homework first.
- ✓ Bring evidence.
- ✓ Ask for critique — not shortcuts.
- ✓ Verify important numbers.
- ✓ Never share passwords or API keys.
- ✓ Think first, ask second.

## Good Examples

- "Here's my analysis. Would Juan buy or pass?"
- "Review this offer in Juan's voice."
- "Would Juan prioritize these leads differently?"
- "Critique this report before I send it to Juan."

## Bad Examples

- "Analyze everything for me."
- "Build my automation."
- "Write my code."
- "Run all my comps."
- "Create my SOP from scratch."
- "Tell me exactly what to do."

## Bottom Line

Ask Juan AI is the CEO's strategic reviewer — not the company's worker. Do the
work, build your recommendation, then use Ask Juan AI to pressure-test your
thinking before it reaches the CEO.
