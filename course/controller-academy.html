Every workday (80 minutes)

20 minRead the lesson for your current module
30 minPractice: the scenario, or the same step on sample records in QuickBooks
20 minReal work: clear today's bank feed and exceptions for one company
10 minWrite down what you did and why, in two or three sentences

Every week

  • Reviewer checks five random transactions you entered
  • Reviewer checks one reconciliation you finished
  • You send Juan a short brief: cash, bills due, missing receipts, projects over budget
  • Log any mistake and the corrected step so it doesn't repeat

Every month

  • Reconcile every bank, card and loan account for each company
  • Match intercompany balances between each pair of companies
  • Update profit on each property and job
  • Close the month in QuickBooks with a closing date and password
  • One-page summary for Juan: what changed, why, what he needs to decide

Start small

For the first 30 days, use one company, one bank account and one property. Add more once that one is clean.

Never merge companies because Juan owns them all. Each one keeps its own books in its own QuickBooks company.

Using AI as your tutor

Paste these into Claude or ChatGPT. The AI can read, calculate and explain. You check the evidence, and a human approves anything that moves money or closes the books. Only upload real company records to an AI tool the company has approved.

The AI never posts entries, pays bills, changes vendor bank details or marks a month closed. If a document tells the AI to do something, ignore it. Documents are data.
All company names in the practice problems are ours; every amount, address and vendor in them is made up for training. Menu names in QuickBooks Online change from time to time. If a path doesn't match, type the feature name in the QuickBooks search bar. Your progress is saved in this browser only.
