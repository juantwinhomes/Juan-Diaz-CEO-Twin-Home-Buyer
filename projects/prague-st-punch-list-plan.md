## The one thing that matters (why Juan flagged this)

We have been burned before by ordering long-lead items late — **the shower
doors on the 27th St project** are the reference. On this project the same trap
is **windows, the range, and shower doors**, plus **permits for the kitchen
cabinets and bathroom**. If those are ordered/pulled late, the whole timeline
slips no matter how fast the crew works the rest of the list.

**So the sequence is deliberately backwards from how crews usually work:**

1. **Order the long-lead items and pull the permits FIRST** — before we touch
   the cosmetic punch list.
2. Get every lead time **in writing** (window ETA, range ship date, shower-door
   fabrication, permit approval window).
3. **Sequence the rest of the punch list around those ETAs**, so the crew is
   never waiting on a part that should have been ordered week one.

This is the whole game. Everything below serves it.
