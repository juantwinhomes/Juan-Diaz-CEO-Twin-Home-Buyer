Contractor flag

Check the floor tile's slip rating before you pay

TCL-2424P: on KZ's list, codes ending in P are usually polished and M matte. A polished 24×24 on a bath floor is slippery when wet and a buyer's inspector may flag it. Ask KZ for the DCOF on the spec sheet. You want ≥ 0.42 wet (ANSI A326.3). If it's lower, switch to a matte option below; they're in the same aisle and similar in price.

California field notes

Build it to pass inspection

  • Pull the permit when plumbing or electrical changes. Unpermitted bath work shows up in buyer disclosures and inspections.
  • Waterproof the surround with a sheet or liquid membrane (Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban) over cement board. No greenboard.
  • Large-format 24×48 needs a flat wall (≤ ⅛″ in 10′), LFT mortar, back-buttering and leveling clips. Keep the offset at ⅓ or less to control lippage.
  • The tub enclosure glass must be tempered safety glazing with the permanent mark (CRC R308.4.5).
  • Pressure-balance or thermostatic shower valve (CPC 408.3). Toilet 1.28 gpf max, lav faucet 1.2 gpm, showerhead 1.8 gpm (CA standards).
  • GFCI on every bath receptacle, a dedicated 20A bath circuit (CEC 210.11(C)(3)), exhaust fan on humidity control (CALGreen 4.506), and Title 24 JA8 high-efficacy LED lighting.
  • Rectified tile at a 1/16″ joint with a color-matched grout (a high-performance or epoxy grout) looks like one slab and won't stain.
