# Design Package — Petaluma (single-story T1-11 flip)

**Example / reference property:** 1464 Sunrise (from Juan's Grok session)
**Market:** Petaluma, CA — Sonoma/Napa wine country
**Status:** APPROVED direction (this is the one Juan finally signed off on)

This is a worked example in the exact format the AI Design Agent should output once a
direction is approved. It also records the direction Juan landed on after ~15 rounds of
refinement.

---

## Market read

Petaluma sits in wine country — relaxed, country, modern-farmhouse feel. The 2026 trend in
Napa/Sonoma flips is **warm charcoal siding** (not stark black), **white trim** to pop the
roof pitch and gables, and a **wood + black accent** at the entry for a high-end look on a
budget. The move here is paint + planks + a new door — realistic for a single-story T1-11
house, no stone and no two-story fantasy.

## History / lesson

Juan reached this direction only after repeatedly rejecting: white on the main siding,
pink/terracotta walls in the entry, stone, and two-story modern renders that didn't match
the real house. **Takeaway for the agent: keep offering genuinely different directions on
rejection — don't defend the first idea.**
