FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers

How do you come up with the offer number?+
We start with what your house would sell for fully fixed up (based on recent nearby sales), subtract the real cost of getting it there, and our margin. We'll walk you through each line — if another buyer beats our number with the same terms, we'll tell you to take it.
Do I pay any fees or commissions?+
No. There's no agent commission, and we cover standard closing costs including escrow and title. The offer you accept is the amount wired to you, minus only whatever you still owe on the property.
Is any house in too rough a shape?+
We've bought houses with failed foundations, fire damage, hoarding conditions, and unpermitted everything. Condition changes the number, not whether we make an offer.
Am I locked in once I get an offer?+
No. The offer is free and stays valid for 14 days. Take it to your attorney, compare it against listing, or just sit with it — you owe us nothing either way.
