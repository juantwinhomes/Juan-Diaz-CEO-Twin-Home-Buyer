The four things to know

  • 1

    Property Leads earns its money back 2.2×. $99,200 spent, 3 homes closed, $215,000 gross revenue. That's about $33,067 in lead cost per deal.

  • 2

    Property Leads is getting more expensive. $341 per lead in May, $497 in August, $539 in September. The cheaper counties were cut, and September had nothing under $500.

  • 3

    Disputes are saving real money. $50,100 credited back on 138 bad leads. Property Leads approves 9 out of 10 disputes we file. 10 more ($4,250) are still waiting on the vendor.

  • 4

    Motivated Leads has cost $13,725 with no revenue entered. One home was acquired (Umland Dr, Santa Rosa) and four more are in progress, but nobody has entered the revenue. Some of its leads are duplicates of Property Leads.

Decisions for you

  1. Enter the revenue for the Motivated Leads deals so we can judge it fairly.
  2. Ask Motivated Leads to stop sending leads we already bought from Property Leads, or pause them.
  3. Decide on a price ceiling for Property Leads, or bring back some cheaper counties.
  4. Chase Property Leads on the 10 disputes still waiting ($4,250), some sent back in June.

Disputes & credits

$50,100 came back from disputes

When a lead is bad (wrong number, not the owner, a duplicate) we dispute it and the vendor credits us. It lowers the bill, but the price per good lead stays about the same.

$47,500Property Leads: 131 of 146 disputes approved (90%). That's 32% of the bill. $4,250 on 10 leads is still waiting on a decision.
$2,600Motivated Leads: 7 of 12 disputes approved (58%). That's 16% of the bill. Nothing pending.

About this data

Where these numbers come from

Sources

  • Property Leads: monday.com board export (Property_Leads_1790608022.xlsx, exported Sep 28, 2026), May 7 – Sep 15, 2026. 390 leads; subtotal rows removed.
  • Motivated Leads: monday.com board export (Motivated_Leads_1788303067.xlsx), May 7 – Aug 9, 2026. 4 subtotal rows removed.
  • Property Leads updated Sep 28, 2026. Motivated Leads unchanged since the Sep 9 compile.

How to read it

  • After disputes = what we actually paid, divided by the leads we kept.
  • As billed = the full invoice, divided by every lead delivered.
  • Dispute approval rate leaves out leads where no dispute was filed.
  • Open months still have disputes waiting on the vendor (September for Property Leads, August for Motivated Leads), so their numbers can drop a little.
  • One $300 Motivated Leads lead in Sonoma has no date, so the months add up to 44 leads while the board has 45.
