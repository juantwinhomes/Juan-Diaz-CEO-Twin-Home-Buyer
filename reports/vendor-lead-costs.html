About this data

Where these numbers come from

Sources

  • Property Leads: monday.com board export (Property_Leads_1790608408.xlsx, exported Sep 28, 2026), May 7 – Sep 15, 2026. 390 leads; subtotal rows removed.
  • Motivated Leads: monday.com board export (Motivated_Leads_1790608606.xlsx, exported Sep 28, 2026), May 7 – Aug 9, 2026. 45 leads.
  • Both boards updated Sep 28, 2026.

How to read it

  • Every number is after disputes. "Paid" is the bill minus credits for bad leads. "Good leads" leaves out the leads that were credited back.
  • Cost per lead = paid ÷ good leads.
  • Reached an offer counts leads that got to an offer, a listing, a contract or a closed deal.
  • Duplicates were found by matching street number and street name across both boards.
