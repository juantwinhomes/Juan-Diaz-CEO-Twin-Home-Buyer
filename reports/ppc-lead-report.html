Google ads bring in deals. Bateman costs more, and the ads are off right now.

1,724leads from Google ads since Jul 2023

The four things to know

  • 1

    The ads pay for themselves. 14 homes bought since 2023. One 2026 deal in San Lorenzo made $127,329, more than everything we spent on ads this year.

  • 2

    Bateman leads cost almost twice as much. $1,227 per real lead with Bateman (Jun–Jul) vs. $677 when we ran it ourselves (Jan–May). Phone calls nearly stopped too.

  • 3

    The ads have been off since Jul 24. Only $312 spent in Aug–Sep, and no new leads are logged for those months.

  • 4

    About 1 in 3 leads is junk. 588 of 1,724 were wrong numbers, spam or tests.

Decisions for you

  1. Turn the Google ads back on?
  2. Keep Bateman, or bring PPC back in-house?
  3. Ask Bateman to explain why their spend numbers don't match our books.

Bateman agency · Jun–Jul 2026

Bateman is getting leads at nearly double the cost

Bateman ran our Google ads for two months. They produced 42 leads and one home we bought, but each real lead cost much more than when we ran it ourselves.

$677per real lead, in-house (Jan–May): $75,836 for 112 leads
$1,227per real lead, Bateman (Jun–Jul): $39,278 incl. fees for 32 leads

What their 42 leads turned into

  • 3 good ones: Robert Gunn (bought Jul 31, $755,000), MK Ding (appointment done), Martha Reece (contract).
  • 21 weren't real sellers: out of our area, wanted full retail price, or already listed.
  • 8 never answered.
  • 10 were our own tests, duplicates or spam.
  • Only 4 phone calls in two months. We used to get 20–30 calls a month.

Their numbers don't match ours. Bateman reports $22,527 spent in June and $16,660 for Jul 1–21. Our books show Google charged $13,943 and $13,610. Ask them to explain the difference.
