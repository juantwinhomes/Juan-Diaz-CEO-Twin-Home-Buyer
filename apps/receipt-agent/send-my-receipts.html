Send My Receipts

Finds receipt and invoice emails in your own Gmail and forwards the ones you pick to receipts@twinhomebuyer.com, where accounting matches them to the company card charges. Nothing else leaves your inbox, and this page shows no one else's data.

Your inbox: shown after you search

Step 1FindPick the dates, usually from Oct 1 to today, and click Find my receipts. The first time, allow Gmail for this page when asked.
Step 2Check the listEvery email that looks like a receipt or invoice is ticked. Untick anything personal or unrelated to the company.
Step 3SendClick Send. Each email is forwarded from your account to receipts@, with its PDF. Emails you sent before are marked so they don't go twice.

Searched: emails with receipt, invoice, payment received, payment confirmation or order confirmation in the subject, or sent from a billing or receipts address. Emails from @twinhomebuyer.com and your own sent mail are left out.

Not included: Anthropic and Claude invoices. Gmail keeps those from Claude's tools, so download them from the Claude billing page and give them to accounting.
