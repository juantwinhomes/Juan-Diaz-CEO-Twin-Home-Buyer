The rules the page follows

These are built in. If one needs to change, ask Seth; most are a single setting.

Deadlines

Receipt: 7 days after the charge
Then the charge counts as late, gets a red edge, and moves up in Most urgent first.
Review: 2 days after a receipt arrives
Receipts left in To review longer show "Received N days ago".
Dates kept: this month and the 2 before
Sep 1 onward in November, Oct 1 onward in December. Older data drops off.

How a match is made

Email receipt
Same amount to the cent, receipt dated up to 10 days before the charge or 3 after, same vendor, and the same card when the receipt shows one.
Possible match
Same amount and dates, but the card shows another name (QuickBooks Payments, PayPal). It waits for Yes, this charge.
Drive file
The amount in the file name, in that card's folder, the same month.
Pro Xtra
A Home Depot charge with no receipt takes the Pro Xtra purchase with the same amount, bought 0 to 6 days before.

What gets flagged

Purchases only
Card payments, transfers and refunds never need a receipt. Mortgage and interest, insurance, taxes, EMD wires, auto and equipment loans, payroll and bank fees are hidden: a statement covers them.
The five flags
No receipt, No property, No approver, Unknown buyer, Personal or unusual. Each one says who to ask and by when.
Who to ask
The card holder, or whoever is filled in as Bought by. Christine, while a card has no holder.

Questions

Questions people ask

If I upload a file that's already there, will it be duplicated?

No. The card report, the P files and the Pro Xtra export each replace the dates they cover. A charge can only have one receipt, and the Upload button disappears once it has one. The one way to get a second copy is in Drive: uploading a receipt that is already filed under a name with no dollar amount.

What if the weekly exports don't get done?

Nothing breaks. More charges stay flagged and get cleared by hand: ask the buyer, then Upload. The P files and Pro Xtra just let the page clear them by itself.

A receipt is already attached in QuickBooks. What do I do?

Click Not needed on that charge. The page cannot see QuickBooks attachments.

Why does a charge say "Not in QuickBooks yet"?

It came from a For review file: the bank sent it, Christine has not posted it. It counts like any charge, and once it posts it counts once.

Why do some Amazon charges never match?

Amazon Business orders placed under Juan's account email his inbox, not accounting@. A Gmail filter in his inbox that forwards Amazon emails to receipts@ fixes it going forward.

The crew posts receipts in Google Chat. Does the page see them?

No. Use Chat's Forward to inbox on the message and forward it to receipts@, or have the crew email the photo to receipts@ with the amount and property in the subject.

Why does a receipt say "Received 9 days ago" in red?

It has waited in To review for more than 2 days. Mark it Good, Follow-up or Not a receipt.
