How this desk works · 10 September 2026
Work 200, not 8,000.

Clarity beats volume every single time. Sales has been rebuilt around one job per seat. Cherry has been pioneering acquisitions for the last couple of months without anyone standing over her, and that stays true. What changes is that she is not also expected to run a floor, and the numbers stop living in someone's head.

Cherry — database and closings

  • Take the roughly 8,000 records down to a live list near 200 that are genuinely workable.
  • Archive the dead ones with a reason: sold, no equity, not interested, bad data. Nothing gets re-worked by accident.
  • Run the text and call automations already built and report what they produced, not that they ran.
  • Keep closing. Commission is unchanged.
  • Jonathan is the technical resource for tracking and automation.
  • Thea trains to full underwriting: comps, repairs, offer number, unassisted.
  • The other two work the live list as investigators, on this board, daily.

Sales manager — the floor

  • Based in Guadalajara. Owns the Mexico team, after-hours coverage and lead distribution.
  • Owns ramping new reps, marketing spend review, and every number on this desk.
  • Paid on what the team produces, not on his own closings.
  • Last line on a missed inbound call. That stops with him.

The daily rhythm

  • Numbers go in daily. Five minutes, not a weekly report.
  • Juan reads them each morning and asks about them. That is the discipline, not the software.
  • Move a property, leave a note. Set a next action or it falls off the earth.
  • If it needs Juan, flag it and he sees it on Today.

Two standing rules

Leads are a company asset that people work, not territory anyone owns. Everyone is fed from the same board because the company needs more than one closer.

Mailer or checkIf a seller mentions a mailer or a check, mark the compliance box on that property and stop the conversation there. It routes to Juan and counsel. No exceptions, no explaining, no apologising.

Not up for debate: boring, consistent, daily. No new tools until this one is being used. Everyone with this page sees the same board, the same numbers and the same notes. It all lives in one company database.
