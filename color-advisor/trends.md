# 2026 Exterior Color Trends — Flip Decision Guide

Researched July 2026. Use this to pick exterior colors for flip properties.
When a sample property photo arrives, run it through the decision framework at
the bottom and pick from these trend-backed options.

## The big picture for 2026

- **Warm earth tones have replaced cool grays.** Gray is no longer the default
  on new construction; warm neutrals photograph better and appeal to more buyers.
- **Sage/muted green is the #1 trend color.** Houzz 2026 data: green-family
  exterior paints up 34% in project saves vs 2024 — more than any other family.
- **Creamy whites replaced stark white.** Warm charcoal replaced blue-gray.
- **Colors of the Year:** Sherwin-Williams *Universal Khaki* (SW 6150),
  Benjamin Moore *Silhouette* (AF-655, burnt umber/charcoal), PPG *Warm Mahogany*.
- Northern California specifically: olive greens, muted sage, smoky green-grays
  paired with stone, wood, black windows, and warm white trim.

## Trend-backed color shortlist (by role)

### Body / siding
| Color | Code | Character | Best on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Universal Khaki | SW 6150 | Warm earthy neutral, 2026 COTY | Safe resale pick, any style |
| Alabaster | SW 7008 | Creamy warm white | Timeless, brightens small homes |
| Balboa Mist | BM OC-27 | Soft greige | Bridges traditional + modern buyers |
| Sea Salt | SW 6204 | Soft green w/ blue undertone | Cottages, landscaped lots |
| Evergreen Fog | SW 9130 | Muted green-gray | Ranch homes, wooded/mature lots |
| Smoky Green | BM CC-700 | Gentle green-gray | Larger facades |

### Trim
- Warm white (Alabaster) against green or khaki bodies.
- Black/bronze window and roofline accents remain strong.

### Front door (highest ROI accent)
- **Deep charcoal, dark warm bronze, warm black** — strongest 2026 resale door colors.
- **Navy blue** — still associated with ~$1,500 higher sale prices.
- Urbane Bronze (SW 7048), Wrought Iron (BM 2124-10) for door/trim/accents.

### Warm-climate accents
- Terra Cotta Tile (BM 2090-30), Southwest Pottery — accent-only, pairs with wood.

## Decision framework for a specific property

Score the property photo on these, then pick:

1. **Fixed elements first** — roof color, stone/brick, driveway. The body color
   must flatter what's not changing. (Warm roof → khaki/greige family;
   gray roof → green-gray family.)
2. **House style** — Ranch: sage/Evergreen Fog with warm white trim is the 2026
   sweet spot. Traditional/cottage: Sea Salt or Alabaster. Modern: greige or
   deeper green-gray with black trim.
3. **Lot & landscaping** — mature trees/landscaping strengthen the case for
   greens; bare lots lean warm neutral (khaki/greige) so the house doesn't
   look unfinished.
4. **Neighborhood comps** — the flip should read current but not be the odd
   house out. If the street is all beige, a muted sage stands out (good);
   a dark moody body may be too far (risk).
5. **Small house rule** — light warm colors (Alabaster, Balboa Mist) make small
   homes look bigger; save deep tones for the door.
6. **Photography test** — warm neutrals and muted greens photograph better than
   stark white or cool gray. Listing photos drive showings.

**Default flip formula for 2026 (Northern California):**
muted sage/green-gray or warm khaki body + warm white trim + warm black or
navy door + black/bronze fixtures + landscape per `color-palette.md`
(redwood, dark mulch, lavender/silver planting).
