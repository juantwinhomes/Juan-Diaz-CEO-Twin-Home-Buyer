# Color Recommendation — 999 Test Ave, Petaluma CA

## Property read (fixed elements first)

- Single-story rectangular ranch, simple massing, one visible window, one entry door — small footprint.
- **Roof:** flat gray composition/shake roof (cool-neutral) — fixed element pointing toward the green-gray family, not khaki/beige.
- **Driveway/walkway:** neutral gray concrete — compatible with either warm or cool body direction, doesn't force a choice.
- **Siding:** currently a warm tan/khaki — already close to the beige family but with a cool gray roof overhead, so a straight khaki match would leave the roof feeling disconnected.
- **Door:** currently a brick-red/terracotta panel — not on the 2026 trend-backed door shortlist.
- **Lot/landscaping:** split condition — mowed green lawn on the left side of the frame, bare dark dirt on the right with no plantings. Landscaping is only half-mature, not fully established.
- **Neighbors:** none visible in frame — no comps to react to.
- **Garage:** not visible in this photo — no street-facing garage door to address.

## Recommended scheme

| Element | Color (name + code + approx hex) | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Body/siding | Evergreen Fog — SW 9130 (~#7D8274) | Gray roof is the dominant fixed element, and rule 1 sends a gray roof to the green-gray family over khaki/greige. This is the 2026 ranch sweet spot and modernizes the current tan without fighting the roof. |
| Trim/fascia/window frame | Alabaster — SW 7008 (~#EDE6D6) | Creamy warm white; repaint the window frame to match rather than leaving it a mismatched tan-gray. |
| Front door | Urbane Bronze — SW 7048 (~#43413C) | Current brick-red door isn't on the trend list; dark warm bronze is a top 2026 resale door color and is the single accent moment on this small, simple facade. |
| Fixtures/house numbers | Matte black | Ties to the bronze door and reads current. |
| Garage door | N/A — none visible in photo | No garage frontage to address here. |

## Runner-up

Universal Khaki SW 6150 body + Alabaster trim + black door — the "max-safety" 2026 neutral. It's rejected here because the gray roof is a stronger signal than the lot's incomplete landscaping, and Evergreen Fog resolves the roof/siding mismatch that khaki would leave unaddressed.

## Free wins

- Landscape the bare dirt patch on the right side of the yard (mulch bed + planting) to match the maintained lawn on the left — an unfinished half-bare lot drags the whole shot.
- Repaint the window frame from its current tan-gray to match the new Alabaster trim rather than leaving a mismatched accent.
- Power-wash/clean the driveway and walkway before listing photos — neutral gray concrete photographs best clean.
- Edge and freshly mow the existing lawn to sharpen the green/dirt boundary before the shoot.
