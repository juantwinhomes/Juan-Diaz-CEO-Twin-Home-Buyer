The leak is mid-funnel, not the mail. 30 sellers said they're interested, but only 3 got an offer (10%) and 0 accepted. The money isn't lost on postage — it's lost between "interested" and "offer made / accepted."

What you're missing — the KPIs that would make ROI real

You measure spend and leads well. These five gaps are why ROI can't be computed today.
GapWhy it matters
1. Closed-revenue attributionNo closing is tied back to a postcard — so the ROI numerator is unknown/$0. This is the #1 fix.
2. Offer-stage trackingYou track leads and "interested," then jump to deals. The leak (30 → 3 offers → 0 accepted) is invisible in the current KPIs.
3. Avg deal profit / breakevenWithout an average spread per deal you can't say if the channel is worth it. Set a target (e.g. break even at 2 deals).
4. Deal-by-list ROIYou track leads by list (liens, tax delinquent, ugly houses) but not which lists produce offers and deals.
5. Contract → close timeMail→contract is 34 days, but no close-cycle metric — so you can't tell if "0 deals" means failing or just too early.
