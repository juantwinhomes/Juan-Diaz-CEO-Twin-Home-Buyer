What Ron told me

First call

  • Madera County Environmental Health has the permit and the Well Completion Report, but the county can take a couple of weeks.
  • The report shows gallons per minute, not a pressure test. A pressure test needs a separate well inspection.
  • He quoted $650 for an above-ground inspection, paid when done by Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, or cash.
  • He had his son pull the report from his office and send it to me.

Second call, after sending the report

  • He says we don't need an inspection since the gallons per minute is on the report, and nothing has changed since 2023.
  • He also said the pump installed may only deliver 10–15 GPM, so the actual flow to the house is unconfirmed.

Still open

2 items before closing

Old well. The report sketch shows an old well next to the new one. The drilling contract included destroying it per Madera County code (filled with sand and a 20 ft concrete seal, $3,750), but we don't have proof yet that it was done. The county should have a well destruction record.

Water test. Done yesterday, September 24, by Dellavalle Laboratory. I'm following up to see if the bacteriological test results can come in this afternoon.
