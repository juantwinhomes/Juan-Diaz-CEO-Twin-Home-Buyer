## Gaps and Flags — fix these before the packet goes out

1. **Both inbound forms are signed by only ONE of two co-trustees.** Denise
   Marks signed; **Sheryl Warnock's lines are blank** on the TDS (pp. 2 and 3)
   and the SPQ (p. 5). The **Equity Track buyer-acknowledgment lines are also
   blank.** Get fully executed copies before passing them through — a
   half-signed disclosure invites exactly the question we don't want.
2. **The rental/occupancy agreement Juan says we have is NOT in what Seth
   has.** Only the move-out agreement came through. Either it exists somewhere
   else (attorney, Brian, Kristine) or the move-out agreement *is* the only
   occupancy document. **Resolve before telling Tabitha we have it.**
3. **"Cash for keys" is the industry term; the document says $10,000 relocation
   paid by check.** Write it the way the contract reads. Don't put "cash" in a
   disclosure when the instrument says check.
4. **Don't write "squatters" in the disclosure.** The signed agreement calls
   them "Tenants," refers to a "supposed landlord/tenant relationship," and says
   the parties *dispute* the validity of the occupation. If our disclosure says
   squatters and our own contract says Tenants, we have contradicted ourselves
   in writing. Disclose the facts — occupants in possession, no rent ever paid,
   validity disputed, terminated by written mutual agreement — and let the
   documents speak. Juan's own instruction: no legal conclusions, no
   characterizations beyond the basic facts.
5. **Smoke detector / water heater strapping.** The prior seller struck those
   certifications. As the seller now, Equity Track owns that compliance at close.
6. **Red tag current status is the whole ballgame.** "We pulled permits and are
   working with the city" is only accurate if that's where it actually stands.
   Get the current status in writing from the City before it goes in a
   disclosure — open, in-progress with permit numbers, or cleared/final.
7. **Proof of the $10,000.** Check copy, bank record, or a signed receipt, plus
   confirmation of keys returned on 7/16. That's what proves the occupancy
   actually ended, not just that an agreement was signed.
8. **PII in the audit log.** The SignNow certificate carries the former
   occupants' personal email addresses and IP addresses. Normal for an e-sign
   cert and it proves execution — but it should go into the transaction file,
   **not** into MLS-attached documents or any public repo.
9. **Accounting:** confirm with Kristine that the $10,000 is capitalized to the
   deal basis, not expensed loose.

## Where This Lands On The Outbound Forms

Equity Track **acquired 6/27/26 and is reselling immediately** — inside the
18-month window. That triggers the TDS Note to C.4/C.5: the transferor must make
additional disclosures on an **SPQ**. Plan on **TDS + SPQ + a supplemental
packet**, not a bare exempt-seller disclosure.

Draft map — **for Tabitha and the attorney to confirm, not final wording:**

| Form line | What goes there |
|---|---|
| TDS II.B — Electrical / Roof / Exterior Walls | Red-tagged electrical, 25-yr patched roof, garage dry rot (carried forward, updated for work done since) |
| TDS II.C.4 / C.5 — work without permits / not to code | Depends on what the red tag was actually for and what we've permitted since |
| **TDS II.C.15 — notices of abatement or citations** | **YES — the red tag** |
| SPQ 5A — documents prepared before your ownership | **YES — attach the prior TDS + SPQ** |
| SPQ 7A / 7F — alterations, and acquired within 18 months | YES. 7F requires the improvements list, contractors over $1,000, and **copies of permits attached** |
| SPQ 10A — water intrusion | Garage roof leak, carried forward |
| SPQ 15C — claims, government hearings affecting the property | Red tag / City proceedings |
| SPQ 17B / 17C — occupancy restrictions, retrofit requirements | Red tag conditions and permit requirements |
| **SPQ 18E / 18F — occupied / previously occupied, and how the tenancy ended** | **This is where the move-out agreement goes.** 18F expressly asks *how* it ended |
| SPQ 19A — material facts | Catch-all; anything the packet doesn't cover |

**Per the reset's non-negotiables, the final disclosure wording goes through the
attorney** — this has legal exposure and it is not a Seth-alone or Juan-alone
call.

## Process — do it this way so nothing sits in email

1. **One Drive folder is the single source of truth:**
   `/Deals/1464 Sunrise Parkway, Petaluma/Disclosures/`
   Nothing lives in a personal folder or an email thread.
2. **Name files so the packet self-indexes:**
   `1464Sunrise_01_MoveOutAgreement_2026-07-15.pdf`,
   `1464Sunrise_02_PriorSellerTDS_2026-06-27.pdf`, etc.
3. **Reconcile Brian's documents first.** Ask him for a list of what he sent and
   when, tick it against the inventory above, and only then tell Tabitha the set
   is complete.
4. **Send as ONE supplemental packet with a cover index**, referenced from the
   TDS/SPQ — not fifteen loose attachments. Loose attachments are how a
   disclosure gets called incomplete later.
5. **Log the delivery** — date, what was sent, to whom, and Tabitha's written
   confirmation of receipt. The delivery record matters as much as the content.
