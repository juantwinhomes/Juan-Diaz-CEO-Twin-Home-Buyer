# Lead Vendor Dispute Rules — PropertyLeads vs MotivatedLeads

> Source: the team's own "Workflow Cheat Sheet" docs embedded in Monday
> (Property Leads board 18392647845; Motivated Leads board 18418843148 —
> its cheat sheet doc id 18426714822/18418843168 family). Captured
> 2026-10-24 after the Twin wrongly applied PropertyLeads logic to
> MotivatedLeads. **The two vendors have different dispute mechanics.**

## MotivatedLeads (board: "Motivated Leads", 18418843148)

- Disputes are **credit requests** and require **PROOF**: "No proof = weak
  dispute." Evidence = call logs, text screenshots, email screenshots, CRM
  notes showing no answer / no response / wrong number / disconnected.
- **Valid grounds:** wrong phone number, disconnected number, invalid
  contact info, duplicate lead, not the property owner, and
  **non-responsive lead WITH documented outreach** (seller never responded
  after documented calls, texts, and email attempts).
- **Workflow:** signs of invalid/non-responsive → Dispute Status
  `Under Review` (keep making + documenting attempts) → `Waiting
  Eligibility` → if more proof needed, keep attempting → `Ready to Submit`
  → submit credit request to MotivatedLeads → `Submitted` → `Waiting
  Decision` → `Approved` (credit/refund granted) or `Declined` (usually =
  not enough proof of contact attempts).
- **Implication:** a lead sitting at contact status "New" is NOT
  dispute-eligible — it has no evidence trail. Working the lead fast and
  documenting every touch is both the sales process AND the refund
  insurance. Speed-to-lead failures forfeit credits for non-responsive
  leads, because there are no documented attempts to submit.
- Board tracks `Dispute Due Date` (typically ~4–5 days after received) and
  a "Days Until Dispute Expires" formula; the cheat sheet says disputable
  leads must be identified **before the dispute deadline** — so outreach +
  documentation must start immediately on arrival.

## PropertyLeads (board: "Property Leads", 18392647845)

- Setting Contact Status `Invalid Lead` **starts the dispute review**;
  cheat sheet says for clear invalids: "Submit dispute immediately."
  More direct flag-and-file than MotivatedLeads; same status ladder on the
  board (Ready to Submit / Submitted / Waiting Decision / Approved /
  Declined).
- Historical recovery discipline was strong (113 refunds won as of July
  2026 report).

## Operating rule for the Twin

Before advising on disputes, check WHICH vendor board the lead is on and
apply that vendor's rules. Finance side: each vendor's charges/refunds are
tracked in separate "Transactions" boards (several workspaces).
