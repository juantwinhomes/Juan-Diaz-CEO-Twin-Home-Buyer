# Lead Vendor Dispute Rules — PropertyLeads vs MotivatedLeads

> Source: the team's own "Workflow Cheat Sheet" docs embedded in Monday
> (Property Leads board 18392647845; Motivated Leads board 18418843148 —
> its cheat sheet doc id 18426714822/18418843168 family). Captured
> 2026-10-24 after the Twin wrongly applied PropertyLeads logic to
> MotivatedLeads. **The two vendors have different dispute mechanics.**

## MotivatedLeads (board: "Motivated Leads", 18418843148)

**OFFICIAL vendor policy** (motivatedleads.com/pay-per-lead-return-policy,
checked 2026-10-24):

- **Window: 5 calendar days** to report an invalid lead ("Our members have
  a window of 5 calendar days to report those that are invalid"). Reports
  are manually reviewed/verified by their team.
- **Credit-eligible reasons (the full official list):**
  1. Mobile home on rented land / in a mobile home park
  2. Vacant land or non-residential lead
  3. Wholesaler lead
  4. Invalid phone number
  5. Duplicate lead through MotivatedLeads (no credit if the original came
     from an outside source)
  6. MLS-listed property
- **⚠️ NON-RESPONSIVE LEADS ARE NOT ON THE OFFICIAL LIST.** The team's
  Monday cheat sheet claims "non-responsive lead with documented outreach"
  is disputable with proof (calls/texts/emails logged). That is NOT in the
  vendor's published policy — at best a courtesy credit. Do not bank
  recovery on it; the cheat sheet should be corrected or the claim verified
  with the vendor in writing.
- **Practical implication:** most eligible reasons are checkable WITHOUT
  the seller answering — MLS status, vacant land/mobile home (property
  records/Zillow), wholesaler (caller ID/research), duplicate (board
  search), invalid number (one call). So day-1 triage per lead =
  records check + MLS check + first call, and file within 5 days.
- The board's `Dispute Due Date` (~received + 4–5 days) matches the 5-day
  window. The cheat sheet's proof discipline (CRM notes, call logs,
  screenshots) still strengthens any dispute — their team verifies claims.

## PropertyLeads (board: "Property Leads", 18392647845)

- Setting Contact Status `Invalid Lead` **starts the dispute review**;
  cheat sheet says for clear invalids: "Submit dispute immediately."
  More direct flag-and-file than MotivatedLeads; same status ladder on the
  board (Ready to Submit / Submitted / Waiting Decision / Approved /
  Declined).
- Historical recovery discipline was strong (113 refunds won as of July
  2026 report).

## Operating rule for the Twin

Before advising on disputes, check WHICH vendor board the lead is on and
apply that vendor's rules. Finance side: each vendor's charges/refunds are
tracked in separate "Transactions" boards (several workspaces).
