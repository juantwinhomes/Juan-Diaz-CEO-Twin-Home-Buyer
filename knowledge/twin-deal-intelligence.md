# Twin Deal Intelligence (TDI)

Captured 2026-07-29 from Juan's brief. TDI is a desktop app that turns Twin
Home Buyer's own history into decisions.

## What it is

Pulls together every data source the business already has — QuickBooks (the
real money), REI BlackBook (all 6,296 leads and their call notes), the Direct
Mail and PPC KPI sheets, Monday.com paid-lead boards, Google Sheets — and
cross-checks them against each other so the numbers we act on are **verified,
not remembered**.

## Why it exists

The business was making decisions on numbers that turned out to be wrong.
Flagship example: **164 Springdale was celebrated as a +$2.7M win and was
actually a −$62K loss.** TDI's job is to make that kind of mistake impossible
by always going back to the books.

## One sentence

Aim every marketing dollar and every caller-hour at leads that look like
proven winners and away from proven losers — using verified data.

## The four end goals

1. **Score any lead at intake — Green / Amber / Red.** A caller types in the
   numbers, the app answers "pursue hard / proceed carefully / walk" based on
   what actually made us money before. The rule it found: **spread beats
   price** — deals with ≥$300K gross spread went 6-for-6 as wins; under $150K
   spread averaged almost nothing and includes our real losses.
   *(Live — the "Score a Lead" tab.)*
2. **Rank marketing channels by real ROI.** Not leads generated — net dollars
   back per dollar spent. PPC is done: **$482K spent → 1.8× return.** Direct
   Mail, SEO, TV, PropertyLeads/ML are the remaining slots. This tells us
   where next month's budget goes.
3. **Explain the conversion cliff — why sellers sign or walk.** Tags and
   economics are done (lost reasons, the 24 cancelled contracts, thin-spread
   math); the deep part — reading the actual call notes to hear why in the
   sellers' own words — is the **Deal Autopsy**, built but not yet run.
4. **Timing and geography.** Speed-to-lead, deal duration, and the county
   map — which markets are hot, which are burned out (high dead/DNC), so mail
   goes where sellers actually respond. Fed by the county-recovery work.

## End state, in Juan's terms

Stop guessing. Every dollar of marketing spend, every hour a caller spends,
and every offer we write is pointed at the pattern that provably made us
**~$4.33M across 47 deals** — and away from the patterns that lost us
**$160K at a time**.
