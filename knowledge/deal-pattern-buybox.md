# Twin Home Buyer — Deal Pattern Profile & Buy-Box

> Source: "Deal Pattern Profile & Buy-Box," compiled from ~160 historical
> property records (Drive settlement statements + expense sheets + public
> records). **All figures are GROSS** (Sale − Purchase − Rehab), before
> financing/holding/commissions. Confidence varies per deal. This is the
> single best strategic document we have on where money actually is.

## Headline Pattern (56 deals with full buy + sell)

- **Win rate ~86%** (48 wins / 8 losses) — *gross basis.*
- **~$12.8M gross profit**, **~$228K average** per completed deal.
- **Median hold ~129 days (~4.3 months)** — genuine quick-flip cadence.
- **Median rehab ~17% of purchase** (mean 21%).

## The Winning Formula (where money reliably is)

- **Oakland / East Bay is the engine:** ~28 Oakland deals, avg sale ~$768K,
  avg gross ~$208K, high hit rate.
- **$600K–$1M sale band is the sweet spot:** 12/13 wins (~$153K avg).
- **Sub-$600K:** 15/17 wins.
- → **The sub-$1M East Bay flip is the lowest-risk, most repeatable play.**
- **Clean smaller markets:** San Leandro, Hayward, Vallejo, Richmond,
  Bakersfield, Rosamond, Lake Elsinore.
- **High-end Peninsula wins are real but high-variance:**
  - 164 Springdale +$2.7M
  - 255 Amber, SF +$1.07M
  - 3612 Hacienda, San Mateo +$938K
  - 1505 Ralston, Belmont +$733K
  - 1416 Solana, Belmont +$457K

## Loss Drivers — Red Flags

**Every loss shares one profile: high purchase price + heavy rehab in
premium/Peninsula markets.**

- 441 Vera St, Redwood City — buy $1.75M, rehab $374K → **−$1.09M (worst)**
- 2832 Eccleston, Walnut Creek — rehab $551K (~70% of purchase) → **−$291K**
- 4715 Bodega, Petaluma — rehab $250K (32%) → **−$210K**
- 663 Pilgrim, Foster City — buy $2.0M, thin spread → **−$141K**
- 410 8th Ave, Menlo Park; 1309 Walnut, San Carlos — sub-scale spread on
  $1.7–1.8M buys.

**RULE:** losses cluster where **rehab > ~25–30% of purchase** and/or **buy >
$1.5M in premium Peninsula cities** (Redwood City, Menlo Park, Foster City,
San Carlos, Walnut Creek).

## Twin's Analysis (Juan's read)

**1. The machine is the East Bay sub-$1M flip. Everything else is a side bet.**
Highest win rate, most repeatable, ~4.3-month turns = capital recycles ~2.5–3x/yr.
This is where acquisition dollars and marketing should concentrate. It's the
system; treat it as the core business, not one of many plays.

**2. Every single loss was predictable — and avoidable.** All 8 losses violate
the buy-box (Peninsula + heavy rehab + >$1.5M). Known losses alone total
~$1.73M (and two more unquantified). The buy-box isn't advice — it's a hard gate
that would have protected ~$2M of gross profit. **The rehab-% rule (>25–30% =
red) is the single most predictive filter** and should be enforced at underwriting.

**3. High-end Peninsula is a barbell, not a strategy.** Biggest wins (+$2.7M) and
worst losses (−$1.09M) both live here. Play it only with discipline: fat margin +
hard rehab cap, and it should be **Juan's personal final-approval seat** (matches
the CEO role design — major risk approval).

**4. The 86% win rate is a GROSS number and is inflated on net.** The source flags
it: thin wins are likely breakeven-to-loss after financing/holding/commissions.
**Until Kristine produces true net per deal, we don't actually know profitability.**
This is the #1 data gap and the highest-value thing to close.
