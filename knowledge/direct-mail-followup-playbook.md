# Direct-Mail "Local Twin" Playbook — Layout + Follow-Up Sequence

> Built for Cherry (acquisitions), July 2026. Replaces the single-touch postcard
> spray with a **5-touch concentrated sequence + multi-channel follow-up**.
> Grounded in `marketing-postcard-kpis.md` (0.12% response, 32.6% interest, but
> 0 acquired — the leak is single-touch mail + follow-up that dies after interest)
> and `deal-pattern-buybox.md` (the East Bay sub-$1M engine).

## Why we're changing it (the data)

- **Interest is real (32.6% of leads), conversion is the leak.** 30 interested →
  3 offers → 0 acquired. Seller notes: "callback made, no answer," offers that
  "insult the seller," sellers list with an agent instead.
- **Single-touch spray.** We mailed once and waited. Direct mail wins on
  *sequence* + *concentration*, not volume.
- **Targeting was diluted.** Only ~15% of mail hit the buy-box; 45% was off-box
  (SF/Peninsula/Central Valley/LA). This sequence runs ONLY on the buy-box.

## Brand rails (non-negotiable — how Juan sells)

- **"It's just building a friend."** Never make a seller feel lowballed or scammed.
- **No overpromise, ever.** No "close in days" lies. Trust is the asset.
- **Lead with local.** Juan is born-and-raised Oakland — our unfair advantage.
- **Protect seller dignity** in every word of copy and every offer conversation.

## Who we mail (concentrate, don't spray)

- Run the FULL sequence on the **~11K East Bay buy-box addresses**, not statewide.
- Priority core: **7 Oakland/Emeryville "Stack" zips** —
  94608 / 94607 / 94612 / 94606 / 94601 / 94621 / 94603 (best-performing drop).
- Same budget, ~5x the touches on the *right* people.

## The 5 Layouts (the sequence)

**Touch 1 — "Real Neighbor" Letter (Day 0).** Handwritten-style, personal look.
> "Hi [First Name] — I'm Juan, born and raised in Oakland. I buy homes right here
> on [Street Name] and around [Neighborhood]. If you've ever thought about selling
> [Property Address] — no repairs, no agents, no pressure — I'd just like to talk.
> Worst case, you get honest info about your options. — Juan, Twin Home Buyer."

**Touch 2 — "Twin" Brand Postcard (Day 14).** Juan's tagline + real photo.
> Front: "I'm the only twin here — but I work twice as fast."
> Back: "Cash offer. Sell as-is. You pick the closing date. A real local buyer,
> not an out-of-state number. Call/text Juan: [#]"

**Touch 3 — "Neighbor Proof" Postcard (Day 28).** Social proof + map pin.
> "I recently helped a homeowner just a few blocks away on [Nearby Street]. Fair
> cash, as-is, on their timeline. If selling [Property Address] has crossed your
> mind, let's talk — no obligation."
> RULE: only real, closed addresses. No fiction.

**Touch 4 — "Your Options" Letter (Day 45).** The anti-lowball card.
> "Three ways I can help with [Property Address]: (1) A fair cash offer, as-is —
> you fix nothing. (2) Pick your own closing date — 2 weeks or 2 months. (3) If
> selling isn't right for you, I'll tell you straight. No pressure, no games."

**Touch 5 — "Still Here" Nurture Card (Day 60, then quarterly).** The long game.
> "No rush — just wanted you to know I'm still local and still here when the time
> is right for [Property Address]. Keep this card. — Juan."
> Most distressed sellers convert on month 3–9, not week 1. Do not drop them.

## The Creative Follow-Up (wrap every mail touch)

| Layer | What | When |
|---|---|---|
| QR / video | Every card has a QR → 60-sec phone video of Juan ("Hi, I'm Juan, actually from Oakland…"). Real face on the mail. | On all 5 |
| Ringless voicemail | Warm 20-sec VM 3 days after each mail touch — "just following up on the letter I sent." | +3 days |
| Text follow-up | Human text, NOT "WE BUY HOUSES CASH." e.g. "Hi [Name], it's Juan — did my letter about [Street] reach you? No pressure, happy to answer questions." | +5 days |
| Speed-to-lead SLA | Any inbound = call back in UNDER 5 MINUTES (Juan's rule). Fixes half the leak alone. | Instant |
| Interested-lead cadence | Day 0 call → Day 2 text → Day 4 call → Day 7 value-touch → Day 14 → then every 2 weeks. Log every touch. | Cadence |
| Door-hanger backup | 7 core zips only: door-hanger to non-responders. Cheap, hyperlocal, high-trust. | Mid-sequence |
