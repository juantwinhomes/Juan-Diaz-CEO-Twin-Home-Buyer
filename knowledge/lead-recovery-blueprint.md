# Juan's Lead Recovery Blueprint — "Dig Up the Gold We Already Paid For"

> Strategic handling of the Lead Recovery function, in Juan's operating style.
> Built for Cherry (acquisitions), July 2026. Grounded in the 90-day cash reset
> (protect cash, no scatter, concentrate), the KPI data (`marketing-postcard-kpis.md`:
> ~$50K spent, 92 leads, ~30 interested, 0 acquired — the leak is follow-up + offer
> feel), and the buy-box (`deal-pattern-buybox.md`: East Bay sub-$1M engine).

## The frame (why this is the #1 cash move right now)

We already spent ~$50K to buy 92 leads and acquired ZERO. The CRM isn't a
graveyard — it's paid-for inventory. **Recovering a dead lead is the cheapest
deal in the business: no new marketing spend, and the first recovered deal pays
for the whole effort.** That's exactly what the cash reset wants — monetize sunk
cost, protect cash, no new venture. This is core, not scatter.

## Strategic decision: pilot, not department (yet)

Run a **lean 60-day pilot**, prove the recovery dollars, THEN scale into the full
org + dashboards. Do not hire a department or build the dashboard cathedral before
a single deal comes back.

- **Team:** 1 Investigator + 1 Specialist. (Jonathan builds the machine; he does
  not work leads by hand.)
- **Scope:** the warm pile only — see triage below.
- **Win condition (set up front):** reactivate a target # of real deals in 60 days.
  Hit it → scale roles + automation. Miss it → the pile's dead; stop and redeploy.

## Triage — concentrate, don't work FIFO

Sort every dead lead by **buy-box + warmth**, work top-down:

| Priority | Profile | Why first |
|---|---|---|
| **P1** | Interested + in East Bay sub-$1M buy-box | Highest odds × highest value |
| **P2** | Bucket B — offer submitted, seller vanished | Deal was alive once; closest to contract |
| **P3** | Interested, out-of-box but clean market | Still warm, lower value |
| **P4** | Ownership/title/probate/trust (Bucket A) | Slow, low hit-rate — do NOT lead with these |

## The cadence machine (speed is the product)

The leak was slow, leaky follow-up. Fix = a fixed cadence + a hard SLA + an
attempt cap so nothing sits in "unknown."

- **Speed-to-lead:** any callback answered in **under 5 minutes** (Juan's rule).
- **Reconnect cadence per lead:**
  Day 0 call → Day 0 voicemail → Day 2 text → Day 4 call → Day 7 value-touch
  (email/letter) → Day 10 alternate numbers → Day 14 letter → relative (callback
  request ONLY) → **disposition.**
- **Attempt cap:** after the full cadence with no contact → mark
  "No Contact After Investigation." No lead stays "unknown."
- **Every touch logged in REI BlackBook.** No exceptions.

## The reconnect = "building a friend," not a chase

Recovered leads went cold partly because offers insulted them. The reopen must
restore dignity, not push. Sample opener (Specialist):
> "Hi [Name], it's [Name] with Twin Home Buyer — Juan's local team here in the
> East Bay. No pressure at all, just circling back on [Property Address] to see
> where things landed and whether I can be helpful. If selling isn't right, no
> problem — I'll close out your file. What's easiest for you?"

Determine ONE of: still interested · needs time · sold already · needs title help ·
ready for inspection → schedule the next step.

## The handoff that actually converts

A recovered warm lead goes to Acquisitions **with a max-offer rule already set**,
so we don't re-lowball the person we just won back. Fixing offer authority is a
prerequisite, not a nice-to-have — otherwise recovery just refills a leaky bucket.
(Ties to Juan's 85% ceiling; >$1–2K exposure routes to attorney/CPA.)

## Build the machine (Jonathan — parallel track, not blocking)

Humans close; the machine makes them faster:
- Auto contact-enrichment from approved sources (PropertyRadar, DealMachine).
- AI summary of each lead's history from REI BlackBook (specialist walks in warm).
- Auto task-creation on status change; John AI voicemail drops.
- Recovery KPI view feeding the weekly scorecard.
Success = system efficiency, NOT leads he personally researches.

## Rhythm & accountability

- **Daily:** one number in the group chat — "X contacted, Y reactivated today."
- **Weekly scorecard** folds into **Kristine's Monday dashboard → Juan's Sunday
  review.** If it's not on the scorecard, it didn't happen.

## Hard rails

- **Compliance:** DNC/TCPA on calls + texts; ~41% of our responders already asked
  to be removed — scrub and honor it. Route calling/texting compliance to the
  attorney before scaling outreach.
- **Never** discuss confidential info with third parties; **never** negotiate with
  relatives; **never** guess ownership; **never** close a task without every
  attempt documented.

## Juan-specific flag

Juan's instinct is to grab the phone himself on hot buy-box leads (he's taken 33
personally). Strategy: **let the Specialist run the cadence; escalate only the
whales to Juan.** The Twin holds this line — CEO time is vision/major deals, not
rep dialing.
