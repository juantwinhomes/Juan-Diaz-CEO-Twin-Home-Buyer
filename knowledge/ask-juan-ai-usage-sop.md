# SOP — How & When to Use Ask Juan AI (Team Guide)

> Twin Home Buyer team guide. Plain language on purpose. Tells the team what the
> tool is for, when to use it, how to ask, and when to stop and get a human.

## What Ask Juan AI IS

Juan's judgment on demand — it thinks and decides the way Juan does, grounded in
the company's real playbooks (buy-box, deal standards, priorities, his voice).
Use it to get "how would Juan look at this?" before you act, or before you bring
something to him.

**What it's NOT:** not Juan's final approval, and not a search engine. It's a
decision-support tool. Big calls still go to a human.

## What it's FOR

- **Deal decisions** — turn raw comp data into a clean note; "buy or pass?"; price
  an offer; check a deal against the buy-box.
- **Seller communication** — draft or rewrite offers, texts, emails in Juan's voice.
- **Strategy & analysis** — listing/market strategy, pattern analysis, "what am I
  missing?"
- **Prioritization** — which leads/deals/tasks to work first.
- **Writing in Juan's style** — reports, updates, team messages, SOPs.
- **A gut-check before you go to Juan** — show up with options + a recommendation,
  not just a question.

## When to ASK vs. just DECIDE

- **Decide yourself:** small, reversible, low-cost things (a $50 lunch, routine
  scheduling). Don't ask permission for what you're empowered to do.
- **Ask Juan AI first:** anything with real money, real risk, a seller on the line,
  or where you want Juan's read before acting.

## When to STOP and escalate to a human (don't rely on the AI alone)

Even after you ask it, these always go to the right person:
- **Legal / contracts** → attorney
- **Financial decisions over $1–2K** → Juan / CPA
- **HR or sensitive people issues** → the right leader
- **Major or high-risk operational calls** → Juan

It's guidance, not the final signature.

## How to ask so you get a great answer

Give it four things:
1. **Situation** — what's going on
2. **The facts / evidence** — numbers, the property, the messages (paste them)
3. **The decision you need** — buy/pass? what to say? which to prioritize?
4. **Any limits** — budget, deadline, ceiling

Then add: *"Separate facts from assumptions, and tell me what's missing."*

## The golden rules

- **Garbage in, garbage out** — feed it real, complete facts.
- **Never paste passwords, API keys, or private credentials.**
- **Its answer is a recommendation, not approval** — real risk goes to a human.
- **It doesn't do the work for you** — you still make the calls and verify comps.
- **If an answer seems off**, ask "what are you basing this on?" and check.

## Good-ask examples

- "Here's raw comp data on [address] — write it as a deal note and tell me if Juan
  would buy or pass."
- "Rewrite this offer to Carmen in Juan's voice — cash, as-is, we handle the tenant."
- "Why isn't this listing selling, and how do we move it fast?"
- "Here are my 40 leads — rank the ones closest to a contract this week."
- "I'm about to tell Juan X — what would he push back on?"

---

**Bottom line:** Use Ask Juan AI to think like Juan and move faster. Use a human
for the final call on anything with money, legal, or people risk.
