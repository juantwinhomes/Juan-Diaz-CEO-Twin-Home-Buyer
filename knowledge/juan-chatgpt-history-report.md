# PART 1 — WHAT I LEARNED ABOUT HOW YOU PROMPT

## The 12 Signature Patterns

**1. Voice-first, stream-of-consciousness.**
Nearly everything is dictated — long unpunctuated run-ons, transcription typos
("chachu PT", "san calros", "sumurise for by and bryan in 100 charactors").
Your intent is always clear even when the words are messy. You also use voice
mode conversationally (the Tagging in REI BlackBook session is a full spoken
back-and-forth).

**2. Context dump IS the brief.**
Your best results come when you paste raw material first — REI BlackBook screens,
call logs, inspection PDFs, text threads with Cherry, harassing emails, closing
statements — then ask a short question on top ("whats wrong here", "what is she
missing", "recap on this").

**3. You record meetings and paste whole transcripts.**
AI is your operations translator: rambling huddle in → clean directive out, with
who-does-what. You did this for lead prioritization (Kixi/ZenFlow/direct mail),
Hubstaff discipline, expense tracking, AI training sessions.

**4. You teach in analogies and expect AI to run with them.**
Gasoline vs. water in the tank (data quality). One brick at a time (incremental
discipline). Recon drone / strike team / command post (Synthflow → sales → data).
Reading the lead like a book. You even tell the team to "take this analogy back
to your Claude."

**5. Terse iterative steering.**
Follow-ups of 1–5 words: "1000", "go", "yes", "neil is with laywers title",
"state that they are great", "san mateo county". You expect full context carried
forward — and it works.

**6. You correct hard when misunderstood.**
"No you don't understand — you ARE me." You repeat the goal in new words until it
lands. You never accept a wrong deliverable.

**7. Send-ready deliverables, always.**
Texts to sellers (Tracy $1.2M offer), cease-contact letters, Yelp/Google review
responses, Slack posts, Spanish crew instructions, basketball hype letters.
You send them nearly verbatim — and you routinely ask for tone variants:
more aggressive / softer / legal undertones / Trump-style / Buffett-style /
Gruden-style.

**8. Your #1 power move: prompts for other people.**
Copy-paste manager bots ("you are the boss, don't let me skip steps, no To-Do =
no pay") for Hannah, Kyier, Arly, Care, Harley. Step-by-step coaches for lost team
members. "Become Juan Diaz" training personas. AI coaching prompts for Charlie's
mailing data. You use AI less as an assistant and more as a force multiplier for
team accountability.

**9. You build AI system instructions iteratively.**
The "Real-Time Lead Scoring & Management" API instruction was drafted and refined
across at least 4 separate chats — tag lists ("Asking for Top Dollar", "High
Budget"), strict output discipline ("tag only in the result, no explanations"),
Google Sheets sync, color-coded 1–100 scoring.

**10. The 4-tool doctrine.**
ChatGPT (drafting/refining) · Claude (long docs/transcripts/images) · Perplexity
(research with sources) · Grok (trends/validation). You drill the team on it
daily: "show off your prompts, one brick at a time."

**11. AI runs your whole life, not just the business.**
Per-player pre-game hype letters for Gavin's team (Guy owns the paint, Ricky
empties the tank), college recruitment emails with Crockett, the 15-year-old
discipline contract, XRP explained in Spanish for the Ramos family, Suno team
anthems, Eichler heating parts lists for your cousin, memorial tributes,
Manila meal costs, mid-century sinks.

**12. Your one recurring leak (now fixed).**
You kept rebuilding the same prompts because they were buried in old chats —
you even asked ChatGPT for "the tagging commands" it had written you weeks
earlier. The fix: **Juan-Prompt-Library.md** (in this same folder) — your 10
proven prompts in one file.
