# 📸 Property Photo Cheat Sheet — for the On-Site Person

**Use this for EVERY property before sending photos to the Designer.**
Shoot the list in order. Don't skip. If something is broken or ugly, we
STILL need the photo — that's how the design gets priced right.

Property address: __________________________  Date: __________
Your name: __________________________

---

## The 3 rules (read first)

1. **Stand BACK.** Show the whole thing, not a zoom. We need the full wall,
   the full room, the full house — not a close-up of one spot.
2. **Landscape mode (phone sideways).** Hold the phone sideways for every
   shot. Good light — open blinds, turn on lights.
3. **When in doubt, take MORE.** Extra photos never hurt. Missing photos
   stop the whole job.

---

## OUTSIDE — shoot these in order

- [ ] **1. Whole front of house** — from the street/curb. House + yard, all in frame.
- [ ] **2. The siding, up close** — walk up, one clear shot of what the walls
      are made of (wood T1-11? stucco? board?) and their condition (peeling? cracks?).
- [ ] **3. Garage door** — full door, straight on. Then one close-up of the handle/condition.
- [ ] **4. Front door + porch** — the entry and whatever plants/steps are right there.
- [ ] **5. All 4 sides of the house** — walk around: front, left, back, right. One photo each.
- [ ] **6. Backyard** — a few wide shots. Include the fence and any trees.
- [ ] **7. The fence** — one shot showing how long it is and what shape it's in.
- [ ] **8. The street** — one photo looking down the block, so we see the neighborhood.

## INSIDE — shoot these in order

- [ ] **9. Kitchen — wide shot** — stand in the doorway, get the whole room.
- [ ] **10. Kitchen — close-ups** — counters, cabinets, the floor, the sink.
- [ ] **11. Living / family rooms — wide** — whole room. **Tilt up to show the
      ceiling** (especially any tall/high ceilings).
- [ ] **12. Floors** — one clear shot of the flooring in each area (carpet? wood? tile? vinyl?).
- [ ] **13. Every bathroom** — full room, plus the vanity and shower/tub.
- [ ] **14. Bedrooms** — one wide shot each.
- [ ] **15. Anything broken or dangerous** — water damage, cracks, exposed
      wiring, electrical panel, anything with a red tag. Close-up + one step back.
- [ ] **16. Trim / doors** — one shot of the baseboards and an interior door.

## MEASURE + WRITE DOWN (photos can't show these)

- [ ] Roughly how many bedrooms / bathrooms: __________
- [ ] Any room with a high/tall ceiling? Which one: __________
- [ ] Rough size of the kitchen (small / medium / big): __________
- [ ] Fence — roughly how many feet, or which sides have it: __________
- [ ] Is the power on? Any red tag from the city? __________
- [ ] Anything else weird or worth flagging: __________

---

## ❌ Don't do this

- ❌ Don't zoom way in on one tiny spot (a screw, a stain) with no wide shot too.
- ❌ Don't shoot dark rooms — open blinds, turn on lights.
- ❌ Don't skip the "ugly" stuff. Broken = we need to see it to price it.
- ❌ Don't send 3 photos of the same wall and nothing of the kitchen.

## ✅ When you're done

Send all the photos + this filled-out sheet to the Designer. That's it —
the AI takes the address, your photos, and the notes and builds the full
design plan and rehab cost. **Nobody has to ask Juan a thing.**
