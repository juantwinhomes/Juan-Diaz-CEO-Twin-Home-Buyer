## Headline

- **Estimated profile:** PI-style Individualist, with Maverick/Venturer traits.
- **DISC:** Producer.
- **Final recommendation:** Keep Juan as CEO, but define the seat as
  **Visionary CEO / Strategic Growth Leader** and pair him with a strong
  COO/Integrator.
- **CEO Fit Score:** 7.5/10 (vision/growth/strategy focus). Lower if ops-heavy.
- **Best department fit:** Executive Strategy / Growth / High-Level Acquisitions.
- **Weakest fit:** Operations, admin, CRM compliance, routine people management,
  detailed process ownership.

## Behavioral Dimensions

| Dimension | Read |
|---|---|
| Dominance | High — direct, decisive, competitive, independent, takes control. |
| Extraversion | Low–moderate — influences when needed; results/autonomy-driven. |
| Patience | Low — high urgency, fast pace, dislikes slow processes/consensus. |
| Formality | Low–moderate — respects structure, challenges rules that slow results. |

## Natural Operating Style

- Moves fast, wants momentum now; makes decisions quickly, expects others to keep up.
- Thinks independently; relies heavily on own judgment; likes control of direction.
- Challenges weak ideas, vague thinking, slow execution; pushes back without strong reasoning.
- Takes calculated risks; pursues bold opportunities.
- May bypass process seen as inefficient; may forget to bring others along.
- Under stress may become isolated or controlling, especially when plans are at risk.

## Strengths

Strategic urgency; bold decision-making; problem solving under pressure; vision &
future orientation; independence; competitive drive; rule-challenging innovation.

## Risks / Watchouts

- Details may get missed (title, contracts, escrow, earnest money, CRM, follow-up).
- May become too controlling → bottlenecks, less team ownership.
- May operate too independently → team feels left out.
- May push faster than the team can execute → priority whiplash without systems.
- May under-communicate the "why."
- May resist routine accountability (checklists, CRM reviews, SOPs).
- Direct style may be misread as harsh or impatient.

## Management Style

Direct, fast, challenging, outcome-focused. Manages best by setting a target and
letting people figure out how to get there.

- **Should:** set clear outcomes; define winning; give decision authority; short
  direct meetings; visible scorecards; let operators own details; focus on
  obstacles ("what's blocking the result?"); hold leaders accountable to numbers.
- **Should not:** micromanage admin; jump into every issue; change priorities
  daily without explanation; give vague direction expecting perfect execution;
  take over others' responsibilities; rely on memory over scorecards; skip
  documentation; personally own all follow-up.

## Ideal Leadership Structure

- **Juan — CEO/Visionary:** vision, strategy, growth, major partnerships, capital
  direction, high-level acquisition strategy, final decisions, big opportunities,
  competitive positioning.
- **Needed COO/Integrator/Ops Lead:** weekly execution, scorecards, CRM
  compliance, department accountability, follow-up systems, SOPs, hiring &
  training process, meeting cadence, deadlines, cross-team comms, implementation.
- **Department leads:** daily team management, lead follow-up quality, call
  reviews, data accuracy, pipeline hygiene, performance reporting, coaching.

## Performance Metrics (as CEO)

- **Revenue/deal:** monthly contracts signed, monthly closed deals, plus deal
  quality/spread and capital raised.
- **Leadership:** fewer repeated operational escalations to Juan.
- **Operational health (inspect, don't own):** CRM next-action compliance, speed
  to lead, follow-up completion, no-response recovery, appointment set rate,
  appointment show rate, offer sent rate, seller nurture pipeline health, task
  overdue count, lead stage accuracy.

## Best Work Environment

Thrives with: clear goals, freedom to decide, fast communication, minimal
bureaucracy, strong operators, metrics-based accountability, direct feedback,
high-stakes opportunities, room to challenge assumptions, a team that executes
without constant direction.

Struggles with: slow committees, repetitive admin, excessive approval chains,
vague ownership, emotional decision-making, open loops, weak operators, unclear
authority, no scorecards, people who need hand-holding.

## Role Design

- **Recommended CEO mix:** 80% vision/strategy/growth/deals/capital; 20%
  leadership review/accountability/decision-making.
- **Avoided mix:** 20% vision; 80% ops/admin/CRM/follow-up/HR/task-chasing.

## Bottom Line

Juan is in the right seat *if* Twin Home Buyer lets him operate as the visionary
and strategic driver. He becomes misplaced only if the business expects him to be
the operator. The solution is not to slow Juan down — it's to build structure
around his speed.
